Flip Match

Find all matching pairs by flipping cards. If they match, they stay face-up. If not, they flip back down.

Download on App Store

Works offline.

What it does

  • Progressive difficulty levels
  • Challenge your friends
  • Train your memory skills

How it works

  1. Tap two cards to flip them over.
  2. If they match, they stay face up.
  3. If they don't match, they flip back down, so remember where they were.
  4. Find all the pairs to finish the level.
