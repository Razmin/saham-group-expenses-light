NovaNext — Option 2 (Bento / App Studio Hub)
===================================================

How to preview locally on your Mac:
1. Open Terminal and `cd` into this folder.
2. Run: python3 -m http.server 8000
3. Open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

Media folders:
- Place your app icons (*.png) in images/icon/ (e.g., logo.png, aiden.png, emma.png, film.png, etc.)
- Place app screenshots in images/screenshots/<key>/iphone_01.jpg (and ipad_01.jpg, etc.)
- Place optional demo videos (*.mp4) in videos/<icon>.mp4
