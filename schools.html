What School Mode changes

One setting, set once by your IT team. Students and teachers don't have to do anything.

No advertising

Ads never load, so nothing in the app pulls students away from the lesson or sends them to outside sites.

No tracking

No advertising identifier is used and no tracking permission prompt is shown to students.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign in to. Students open the app and start exploring, and their work stays on the device.

What students can explore

Useful in earth science, geography and general science lessons.

  • Daylight hours at any latitude on any date, to see why seasons and polar days happen.
  • Earth's orbit around the Sun, and how the tilt of the Earth shapes the year.
  • The Moon's orbit around the Earth.
  • The solar system, with information about each planet.
  • Sunlight angle, to see why a high Sun warms the ground more than a low one.

Setup guide for school IT

About five minutes, using the Apple School Manager and MDM you already have.

Add TerraSol to your devices

In Apple School Manager, open Apps and Books, search for TerraSol and get the app for your location. Then assign it to your devices through your MDM, the same way you deploy any other app.

Please deploy it as a managed app. iOS only delivers app configuration to managed apps.

Get your school token

Each school gets its own access token. Contact us with your school or district name, your time zone and the date you want access to run until. We will send you a token, usually within a few days.

The token is a single line of text. It is for your school only, so please don't share it outside your organization.

Add the app configuration

In your MDM, open the settings for TerraSol and find the section called App Configuration, Managed App Configuration or similar. Add this one key:

KeyTypeValue
schoolTokenStringYour school token

If your MDM asks for XML instead of a form, paste this:

<dict>
    <key>schoolToken</key>
    <string>PASTE-YOUR-TOKEN-HERE</string>
</dict>
Key names are case-sensitive: use schoolToken exactly. Paste the token exactly as we sent it, on one line, with no extra spaces or line breaks. If your MDM offers a type menu, choose String.

Check it on a test device

Push the configuration to one iPad first. Open TerraSol: you should see no ads and no tracking permission prompt. Then roll it out to the rest of your fleet.

Tokens expire. Your token works until the date we agreed with you. After that, TerraSol goes back to the regular App Store version, with ads. Contact us before the date and we'll send a new token to update in your MDM.

Restart the app after any change. School Mode is read when TerraSol starts. If you turn it on or off while the app is open, have staff force-close TerraSol (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. Until then, the app may still show ads or behave as before.

Questions from IT teams

What happens if the token is missing, wrong or expired?

TerraSol behaves like the regular App Store version, which includes ads. School Mode is only on when schoolToken holds a valid token that hasn't expired, so if you see ads on a school device, check that the app is deployed as a managed app, that the key is spelled exactly as above, that the token was pasted completely with no extra spaces, and that TerraSol was force-closed and reopened after the configuration was pushed. If it still doesn't work, or your token has expired, contact us.

Does TerraSol collect student data?

TerraSol has no accounts, and what students do in the app stays on the device. In School Mode, advertising and cross-app tracking are turned off. Our Privacy Policy describes School Mode, and the rest of the policy describes everything the app can collect, and we're happy to answer your district's privacy questions directly.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. TerraSol runs on the device, so it works on school networks with restricted access.

Which devices does it run on?

iPad and iPhone. The App Store listing shows the minimum iOS version. TerraSol is designed to work especially well on iPad in landscape.

Can TerraSol be adapted to our curriculum?

Possibly. Tell us what you are teaching and we will say honestly whether it fits the app and what it would involve. We do not promise features in advance: anything we build for your school is agreed with you first, including timing and cost. Get in touch to start the conversation.

What does the school pay?

TerraSol is free on the App Store, and School Mode doesn't change that. If you'd like to discuss a bulk arrangement, or something built for your curriculum, get in touch. Anything beyond the app as it is today is agreed with you first, including any cost.

My MDM doesn't show an app configuration option

Most MDMs (Jamf, Mosyle, Intune, Meraki and others) support managed app configuration, but the menu name varies. Search your MDM's help for "managed app configuration" or "app config". If you get stuck, send us the name of your MDM and we'll help you find the right place.
