Frequently Asked Questions

The most common things people write in about.

How do I restore a purchase or remove ads I already paid for?

Open the app and look for a "Restore Purchases" option inside Settings → Remove Ads. This re-applies any purchase tied to your Apple ID at no extra charge. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

An app crashed or isn't working right

First, make sure the app and your iOS version are fully up to date. If it still happens, try restarting your device. Still stuck? Pick your app below and send us your device model and what you were doing when it happened.

How do I delete my data?

NovaNext apps store your content (lists, scores, files, etc.) directly on your device, not on our servers. Deleting the app from your device removes that data permanently. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do NovaNext apps work offline?

Yes — almost everything runs locally on your device, so most apps work fully without an internet connection. A connection is only needed for online multiplayer, live film databases, or ads.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

Our apps are built natively for iPhone and iPad. Each app's App Store listing shows its minimum iOS version — we recommend keeping your device updated to the latest iOS release.
